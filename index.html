Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you register for NX-913, including your name, email address, university, and technical skills.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to manage your participation, communicate event updates, and share resume data with Gold/Platinum/Title sponsors for recruitment purposes (as per the tier benefits).

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share resume data only with authorized sponsors who have signed data usage agreements.

Code of Conduct

NX-913 is dedicated to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, age, or religion.

1. Expected Behavior

  • Be respectful and collaborative.
  • Hack on your own code. Plagiarism is not tolerated.
  • Respect the venue and equipment.

2. Harassment

Harassment includes offensive verbal comments, deliberate intimidation, stalking, following, harassing photography or recording, sustained disruption of talks, and unwelcome sexual attention.

3. Consequences

Participants asked to stop any harassing behavior are expected to comply immediately. Organizers may expel offenders from the event without warning.
